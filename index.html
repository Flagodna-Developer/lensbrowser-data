What's inside

Privacy isn't a setting.
It's the default.

Dual-layer Ad Blocking

Blocks ads at both the network connection level and via JS injection — two layers so nothing sneaks through. Five block categories: Tracking, Advertising, Analytics, Low-score domains, and Ad Blocker patterns.

Connection + JS

Anti-Fingerprinting

Hides User-Agent and other browser fingerprint signals when Privacy Mode is enabled. Websites can't build a profile on you.

Privacy Mode

Auto-Clean on Close

All browsing history, cookies, and cached data are automatically wiped the moment you close the app.

Zero Trace

Threat Detection

Flags dangerous, gambling, and suspicious websites with a modal warning before the page even fully loads.

Pre-load Warning

Trusted & Blocked Domains

Whitelist domains you trust, or hard-block ones you don't. View your full blocked content log with domain and URL details.

Manual Control

Flexible Toolbar

Place the navigation toolbar at the top or bottom of the screen — whichever suits how you hold your phone.

Your Layout

Long-Press Shortcuts

Long-press any URL in the toolbar to copy it instantly. Long-press images to get a native contextual menu with multi-language labels.
